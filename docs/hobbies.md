# Hobby collection

The catalog contains three objects for each of twenty hobby themes. This is a design selection of widely practiced hobbies, not an asserted worldwide top-20 ranking. Comparable worldwide participation figures using one definition of “hobby” were not available in the sources reviewed. Regional surveys use different definitions and populations, so they should not be combined into a numerical global ranking.

The selection balances common home-based activities with equipment that can personalize a flat: cycling, running, strength training, yoga/Pilates, reading, gaming, music, photography, painting/drawing, sewing/knitting, cooking/baking, gardening, hiking/camping, fishing, travel, board games/chess, film/home cinema, dance, collecting and pets/aquariums.

Sources consulted:

- [Nielsen Consumer & Media View, September 2025](https://www.nielsen.com/news-center/2025/from-road-trips-to-recipes-nielsen-data-shows-aussies-are-choosing-simple-pleasures-over-big-splurges/): Australian participation in cooking, reading, gardening, travel and outdoor leisure.
- [CivicScience, budget-friendly hobbies](https://civicscience.com/how-americans-are-redefining-free-time-through-budget-friendly-hobbies/): U.S. interest in movies/TV, reading, outdoor activities and cooking/baking.
- [Statista Consumer Insights chart](https://www.statista.com/chart/30496/share-of-respondents-who-say-socializing-is-one-of-their-hobbies/): cross-country differences and U.S. interest in cooking/baking, pets, gaming, outdoor activities and reading. The search excerpt was available; the full page returned an access restriction.

The remaining themes provide recognizable home equipment and breadth within arts, crafts, sport and entertainment. Objects are static design props; a treadmill or sewing machine does not run a separate exercise or crafting simulation.
